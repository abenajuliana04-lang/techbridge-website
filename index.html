30-Day Practical Internship

Learn by shipping, not by watching

The internship runs for 30 days and is built entirely around practical, project-based tasks — the kind of work you'd actually be asked to do on a team.

Project-based

Every task produces something real: a website, a dashboard, a working feature — not a worksheet.

Progressive difficulty

Tasks start at beginner level and build steadily toward more advanced, independent work.

Recommendation letter

Interns who complete the program successfully may receive a recommendation letter based on their performance.
